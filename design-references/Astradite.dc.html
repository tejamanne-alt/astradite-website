Products

Three things in orbit.

Everything we ship is our own product first. Each one leaves behind infrastructure the next one starts from.

01 LIVE

DineOnTap

Scan. Order. Know your customers.

A table orders, eats and leaves — and most restaurants learn nothing from it. DineOnTap turns a QR code on the table into a live menu, a kitchen ticket, and a picture of who keeps coming back.

02 LIVE

Wellness Axis

Health conditions, decoded across food, activity and sleep.

You can log everything and still not know what any of it did. Wellness Axis runs your food, activity and sleep through a knowledge graph and projects a signed impact score against the conditions you actually live with.

03 IN PROGRESS

Medico Nexus

Every case, a colleague away.

Medicine is a collective discipline, practiced alone. Medico Nexus is a network where a clinician can pose a de-identified case and reach the peers who have already seen it. In progress.

How we work

Four moves, repeated until it holds.

  1. 01

    Observe

    We start inside the problem, not the pitch — the constraints, the users, the systems already in the room.

  2. 02

    Architect

    A model of the domain before a line of product code. Data first, interface second, never the reverse.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Something real in front of real users early, then tightened in short loops against how they actually use it.

  4. 04

    Compound

    What one product proves, the next one inherits. Nothing is rebuilt twice.
